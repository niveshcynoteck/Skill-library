# Post-Independence India (1947–Present)

## Constitution & Founding (1947–1950)

India became independent on **15 August 1947**. The Constituent Assembly's Drafting Committee, chaired by **Dr. B.R. Ambedkar**, produced the Constitution, **adopted on 26 November 1949** and **in force from 26 January 1950** (Republic Day). B.N. Rau served as constitutional advisor.

## Integration of Princely States (1947–1949)

Over **560 princely states** had to choose between India, Pakistan, or independence. **Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel**, aided by **V.P. Menon**, used the Instrument of Accession to bring most states into India before 15 August 1947.
- **Junagadh:** annexed 9 November 1947; confirmed by a February 1948 plebiscite.
- **Hyderabad:** annexed via military action ("Operation Polo") on **17 September 1948**.
- **Jammu & Kashmir**'s accession (October 1947) led directly to the first India–Pakistan war.

## Major Wars

| Conflict | Dates | Outcome |
|---|---|---|
| First Kashmir War | 27 Oct 1947 – Jan 1949 | Ceasefire line established in Kashmir |
| Sino-Indian War | 20 Oct – 21 Nov 1962 | China retained Aksai Chin; Line of Actual Control formed |
| Second Kashmir War | Aug–Sep 1965 | Ceasefire; Tashkent Declaration (Jan 1966) |
| Bangladesh Liberation War | 3–16 Dec 1971 | Decisive Indian victory; Bangladesh created |
| Kargil War | 3 May – 26 Jul 1999 | India recaptured all posts |

## Political Milestones

- **Nehru era (1947–1964):** Planned economic development, non-alignment, state-building.
- **The Emergency (25 June 1975 – 21 March 1977):** Declared under Article 352 on the advice of PM **Indira Gandhi**; suspension of civil liberties, press censorship, opposition arrests, the 42nd Constitutional Amendment. Lifted after Congress's 1977 election defeat.
- **Coalition era (1989–2014):** United Front under H.D. Deve Gowda (1996–97) and I.K. Gujral (1997–98); BJP-led NDA under **Atal Bihari Vajpayee** (1999–2004); Congress-led UPA under **Manmohan Singh** (2004–2014).
- **2014–2024:** BJP under **Narendra Modi** won majorities in 2014 and 2019; the 2024 election produced a coalition-dependent third term (BJP-led NDA: 293/543 seats; BJP alone 240, short of the 272 majority).

## Economic Milestones

- The **Planning Commission** was established 15 March 1950; the **First Five-Year Plan (1951–56)** prioritized agriculture, targeting 2.1% GDP growth and achieving ~3.1%. Successive plans continued until the Planning Commission was replaced by NITI Aayog in 2015.
- **1991 Liberalization:** A severe balance-of-payments crisis forced PM **P.V. Narasimha Rao** and Finance Minister **Manmohan Singh** to launch the New Economic Policy, dismantling the "License Raj" via Liberalization, Privatization, and Globalization (LPG) reforms.

## Notable Modern Developments

- **Nuclear tests:** **Operation Smiling Buddha** (18 May 1974, under Indira Gandhi) — India's first nuclear test; **Pokhran-II/Operation Shakti** (11 & 13 May 1998, under Vajpayee) — five tests, establishing India as a declared nuclear power.
- **IT industry growth:** IT's GDP contribution rose from 0.38% (1991) to ~7–8% by the late 2010s; industry revenue grew from $167bn (FY18) to ~$297bn (FY25).
- **Recent economic shifts (2024–2025):** India cited as the world's fastest-growing major economy (~6.4–7.6% growth projected for 2025–26), ranked the world's **4th or 5th largest economy** depending on methodology.
