# Overview

The AI landscape is increasingly divided along a key dimension: **autonomy**. This report compares **agentic AI** (systems that plan, decide, and act toward goals with minimal human input) against **non-agentic AI** (systems that respond only when directly instructed, task by task). Industry sources — including [IBM](https://www.ibm.com/think/topics/agentic-ai), [MIT Sloan](https://mitsloan.mit.edu/ideas-made-to-matter/agentic-ai-explained), and [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AI_agent) — broadly converge on this distinction, though exact terminology varies by vendor.

> **Note on sourcing:** Some figures in this report (e.g., market-size projections, "70% of organizations by 2025") come from industry blogs rather than peer-reviewed or official sources, and should be treated as industry estimates rather than verified facts.
