# Advantages and Limitations

## Agentic AI

**Advantages**
- Reduces human intervention and transaction costs (searching, communicating, coordinating)
- Handles complex, multi-step, and unpredictable scenarios
- Operates continuously (24/7) without fatigue
- Learns and improves over successive interactions

**Limitations**
- Reliability issues — "rogue" agent behavior or biased decisions with real-world consequences
- Struggles with exception-handling tasks that humans find trivial
- Expanded system/tool access increases the cybersecurity attack surface
- Accountability gaps — unclear responsibility when an autonomous action causes harm
- Poor explainability — agent decision paths are often not well understood
- Higher implementation cost and governance overhead

*(Source: [IBM via MIT Sloan summary](https://mitsloan.mit.edu/ideas-made-to-matter/agentic-ai-explained))*

## Non-Agentic AI

**Advantages**
- Lower cost, simpler to deploy
- Predictable, controllable behavior
- Easier to audit and understand
- Fewer ethical/accountability concerns

**Limitations**
- Higher long-run operational cost due to limited automation
- Cannot adapt to unexpected situations
- Inefficient for complex, multi-step workflows
- Prone to human error in repetitive manual steps
