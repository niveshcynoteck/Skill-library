# Overview

The 2026 FIFA World Cup was the 23rd edition of football's premier international tournament, and a historic one: the **first World Cup jointly hosted by three nations** — the **United States, Mexico, and Canada** — and the **first with an expanded 48-team format**.

| Detail | Information |
|---|---|
| **Dates** | 11 June – 19 July 2026 (39 days) |
| **Hosts** | United States, Mexico, Canada |
| **Teams** | 48 (expanded from 32 in 2022) |
| **Groups** | 12 groups of 4 |
| **Total matches** | 104 |
| **Opening match** | Mexico vs. South Africa, Estadio Azteca, Mexico City (11 June) |
| **Final** | Spain 1–0 Argentina (a.e.t.), MetLife Stadium, East Rutherford, NJ (19 July) |
| **Champion** | Spain (2nd title, first since 2010) |
