# The Final & Result

- **Spain 1–0 Argentina** (after extra time), 19 July 2026, MetLife Stadium.
- **Ferran Torres** scored the winning goal in the 105th minute.
- Argentina's **Enzo Fernández** was sent off in stoppage time before the winner.
- This is Spain's **second World Cup title** (first since 2010) and denied Argentina back-to-back titles as defending champions.
