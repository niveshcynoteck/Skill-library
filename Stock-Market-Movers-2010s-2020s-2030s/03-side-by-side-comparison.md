# Side-by-Side Comparison

| Dimension | 2010s (Actual) | 2020s (Actual, 2020–2026) | 2030s (Forecast) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Dominant theme** | Post-crisis recovery, FAANG era | COVID shock → inflation → AI boom | AI productivity diffusion, demographic/energy shift |
| **Top movers** | Facebook, Amazon, Apple, Netflix, Google | Nvidia, Apple, Microsoft, Alphabet, Amazon, Meta, Tesla | Broader set as AI moves from "adoption to deployment"; power/energy infrastructure |
| **Macro driver** | Zero rates + QE | COVID stimulus → rate hikes → AI capex boom | High starting valuations; AI capex sustainability in question |
| **S&P 500 character** | +300–400%, steady bull run | Volatile: crash, bear market, then narrow AI rally | Modest, uncertain — historically low percentile of expected returns |
| **Concentration** | FAANG ~14% of index by 2019 | Top 10 ~40% of index by 2026 | Unknown — could broaden or concentrate further |
| **Key risk** | Inequality in who benefited | AI bubble concerns, mega-cap concentration | Forecasts themselves — wide error bands |
