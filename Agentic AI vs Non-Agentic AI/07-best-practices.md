# Best Practices (from industry guidance)

- Treat agenticness as a **spectrum**: match the autonomy level to the task's risk tolerance rather than defaulting to full autonomy.
- Keep a **human-in-the-loop** at higher-risk decision points (per the L2/L3 "approval" pattern in the autonomy spectrum).
- Invest in **governance, monitoring, and explainability** tooling before granting agents broad tool/API access — cybersecurity and accountability risks scale with autonomy.
- For simple, well-defined, high-volume tasks, non-agentic (rule-based or single-call) systems remain more predictable, auditable, and cost-effective.
