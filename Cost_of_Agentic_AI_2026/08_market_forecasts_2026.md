# Market Size & Spending Forecasts for Agentic AI (2026)

## The Core Problem: Definitions Vary Wildly

There is no single agreed-upon "agentic AI market." Analyst estimates for 2026 range from roughly $7–11 billion to over $200 billion, depending entirely on scope — standalone agentic AI software/platforms vs. all "agentic capabilities" embedded across enterprise applications and IT spending. Treat any single headline number with caution unless its scope is stated.

## Gartner

- **Standalone agentic AI platform market:** roughly $7–8.5 billion in 2025/2026 (narrow scope).
- **Broader "agentic AI spending"** (capabilities embedded across enterprise software): **$201.9 billion in 2026**; purpose-built AI agent software growing from $86.4B (2025) to **$206.5B (2026)** — roughly +139% year over year.
- Longer-term: broader spend compounding at ~119% CAGR, from $15B to $753B by 2029.
- **40% of enterprise applications** will integrate task-specific AI agents by end of 2026, up from under 5% in 2025.
- Caution flag: **over 40% of agentic AI projects will be canceled by end of 2027** due to cost, unclear value, or inadequate risk controls.
- Most recent Gartner note (July 2026): **$234 billion in enterprise application software spend** is "at risk" from agentic AI disruption.

## IDC

- Agentic AI already represents an estimated **10–15% of enterprise IT spending** in 2026.
- Longer-range: agentic AI will account for over 26% of worldwide IT spending by 2029, with total worldwide AI IT spending reaching $1.3 trillion in 2029.
- Adoption trajectory: half of enterprises will use AI agents by 2027; 45% will orchestrate AI agents at scale by 2030.

## McKinsey

- 88% of organizations use AI in at least one business function, but only **23% have scaled agentic AI anywhere**; 62% are still experimenting, with fewer than 10% scaling in any single function.
- Only about 30% of organizations have reached a higher maturity level (3+) on agentic AI governance/controls.
- McKinsey does not publish a dollar market-size figure comparable to Gartner/IDC/Bloomberg — its contribution is adoption-maturity data.

## Bloomberg Intelligence

- Generative AI overall market projected to reach **$2.3 trillion by 2032** (up from a prior $1.8T forecast).
- Hyperscaler AI capex approaching **$750 billion in 2026** alone.
- Agentic AI deployments across consumer and enterprise use cases estimated at approaching $286 billion.
- At just 5% task-penetration, AI-agent "digital labor" implies a $2.3 trillion global TAM, including $452 billion in the US.

## Key Discrepancy Summary

| Scope | 2026 Estimate | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Standalone agentic AI software | ~$7–11B | Gartner (narrow), market-research firms |
| Purpose-built AI agent software | ~$206.5B | Gartner |
| Broad "agentic AI spending" | ~$201.9B | Gartner |
| Agentic AI share of enterprise IT spend | 10–15% | IDC |
| AI-agent "digital labor" TAM (5% penetration) | $2.3T (global) | Bloomberg Intelligence |

The 20–30x gap between the narrow and broad figures is the single biggest inconsistency across sources and should be flagged explicitly whenever these numbers are used.
