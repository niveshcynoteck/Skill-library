# Enterprise Cost Breakdown for Agentic AI Adoption

## Licensing / Subscription Models

Enterprise agentic AI pricing is fragmenting away from classic per-seat SaaS:

- **Per-seat/agent-seat:** some vendors sell "agent seats" — a flat fee per autonomous agent deployed, priced against the value of labor displaced.
- **Usage-based:** metered by tokens, tasks, API calls, or agent-hours/workflow executions.
- **Hybrid (dominant model):** a base subscription plus a variable usage layer. Industry pricing trackers report seat-based-only pricing fell from ~21% to ~15% of SaaS vendors in about a year, while hybrid models rose from ~27% to ~41%. IDC is cited as forecasting ~70% of software vendors moving away from pure per-seat pricing by 2028.

## Implementation and Integration Costs

Vendor/consultancy estimates (not audited industry statistics), broadly convergent across sources:

- Proof of concept: ~$15K–35K
- MVP/pilot: ~$25K–60K
- Single-agent production deployment: ~$30K–150K
- Multi-agent, enterprise-grade deployment: ~$150K–400K+ (regulated industries add a 20–40% compliance premium)
- Global/complex enterprise rollouts: can exceed $1 million
- Ongoing run costs (hosting, monitoring, tuning): ~$2,000–10,000/month per deployment

A recurring theme: **integration and compliance/governance work, not the AI model itself, dominates spend** — several sources estimate 40–60% of total project cost goes to system integration and compliance layers.

## Reported Spend Figures (2026)

**Market-level (Gartner):**
- Worldwide AI spending forecast to reach **$2.59 trillion in 2026**, up 47% year-over-year.
- **AI agent software spending specifically forecast at $206.5 billion in 2026**, growing to $376.3 billion in 2027 — the fastest-growing AI subcategory (~139% YoY growth).
- **40% of enterprise applications** projected to feature task-specific AI agents by end of 2026, up from under 5% at the start of the year.
- IDC is cited as estimating agentic AI already represents **10–15% of enterprise IT spending** in 2026.

**Adoption vs. ROI (McKinsey):** 23% of organizations are scaling an agentic system in at least one function, 39% experimenting (~62% combined engagement). **No single business function had more than ~10%** of organizations reporting scaled agent use, and only ~39% report any EBIT impact attributable to AI. Gartner separately projects **over 40% of agentic AI projects will be cancelled by 2027** due to unclear ROI.

**Case studies with disclosed figures:**
- Klarna's customer-service AI agent reportedly saved **$60 million**, handling workload equivalent to 853 employees.
- A consultancy case study cites a client realizing $900K+ in 2026 savings, with $9M+ projected for FY2027.
- A logistics case study cites ~$980K in annual support-cost reduction from an agentic deployment.

## Caveat

Reliable, audited "typical enterprise spend" figures are not widely available; most implementation-cost ranges come from vendor/consultancy marketing content rather than named primary surveys and should be treated as directional estimates. The clearest verifiable figures are the Gartner market-level forecasts and McKinsey's adoption/ROI percentages.
