# Host Cities & Stadiums

16 host cities across three countries.

| Country | Cities (Stadiums) |
|---|---|
| **United States (11)** | Atlanta (Mercedes-Benz Stadium), Boston (Gillette Stadium), Dallas (AT&T Stadium), Houston (NRG Stadium), Kansas City (Arrowhead Stadium), Los Angeles (SoFi Stadium), Miami (Hard Rock Stadium), New York/New Jersey (MetLife Stadium), Philadelphia (Lincoln Financial Field), San Francisco Bay Area (Levi's Stadium), Seattle (Lumen Field) |
| **Mexico (3)** | Guadalajara (Estadio Akron), Mexico City (Estadio Azteca), Monterrey (Estadio BBVA) |
| **Canada (2)** | Toronto (BMO Field), Vancouver (BC Place) |

*Note: One source claimed Washington D.C. was in the running but "snubbed" from final host selection — this reflects earlier bidding-process reporting, not a contradiction.*
