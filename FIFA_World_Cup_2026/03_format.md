# Format

- **48 teams** divided into **12 groups of 4** (drawn from four pots of twelve).
- Group stage: each team plays 3 matches.
- **Top 2 teams from each group + the 8 best third-placed teams** (20 total) advance to a new **Round of 32** — an extra knockout round compared to the previous 32-team format.
- From there: Round of 16 → Quarterfinals → Semifinals → Final (and third-place match).
- Total of **104 matches**, up from 64 in 2022.
