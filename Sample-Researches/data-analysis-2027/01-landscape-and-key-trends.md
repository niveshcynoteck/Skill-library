# 1. Landscape & Key Trends

2027 is being shaped by one dominant shift: **analytics is moving from describing what happened to driving what happens next**. Analyst firms, vendors, and market data all point in the same direction — AI-first, real-time, and decision-focused analysis.

## Key trends defining 2027

### 1. Decision intelligence replaces dashboards as the end goal

- Analytics is judged less by reports and more by how well it supports predictive, prescriptive, and automated decisions. [1]
- Gartner predicts that by 2027, **50% of business decisions will be augmented or automated by AI agents** for decision intelligence. This combines data, analytics, and AI into automated decision flows. [2]
- By 2027, augmented analytics is expected to evolve into **autonomous analytics platforms that fully manage and execute 20% of business processes**. [3]

### 2. Real-time analytics has become the baseline

- Streaming analytics (processing data in milliseconds) is now a "baseline expectation" in finance, e-commerce, logistics, and healthcare — not a differentiator. [4]
- Market sizing: the streaming analytics market is projected to grow from **USD 7.74 billion (2019) to USD 52.19 billion by 2027, at a CAGR of 26.8%**. [5]
- Drivers include IoT (30+ billion connected devices expected by 2027), 5G, and event-driven architectures. [6]

### 3. Data volume keeps exploding, but trust is the bottleneck

- Data and analytics leaders estimate organizational data volumes grow **~25% annually**. Yet they say **26% of their data is "untrustworthy"**, and 54% of business leaders are not fully confident the data they need is accessible. [7]

### 4. Governance and AI readiness are no longer optional

- 62% of organizations say a **lack of data governance is the main data challenge inhibiting AI initiatives**. [8]
- Gartner links better data semantics/metadata to **up to 80% higher GenAI model accuracy and 60% lower costs** by 2027. [2]

### 5. The market itself is growing fast

- The global data analytics market is projected to grow from roughly **USD 104 billion (2026) to USD 496 billion by 2034** (Fortune Business Insights, as cited by TechDogs). [4]
- Analytics-as-a-Service was forecast to add **USD 34.11 billion between 2023 and 2027** (CAGR ~23%). [9]
