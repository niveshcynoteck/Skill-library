# 5. Skills & Careers

## Strong demand, changing job content

- The **World Economic Forum's Future of Jobs Report 2025** ranks **Big Data Specialists**, **AI & Machine Learning Specialists**, and **Data Analysts & Scientists** among the fastest-growing roles to 2030. Overall it projects **170 million jobs created and 92 million displaced by 2030 (net +78 million)**. [26]
- The WEF names **AI and big data** the fastest-growing skill, and expects **39% of workers' skills to change by 2030**. [26]
- US Bureau of Labor Statistics projects ~**23% job growth** for operations research analysts by 2032 (figure widely cited, e.g., by 365 Data Science). [27] *(The BLS page itself returned HTTP 403 to our validator — bot protection; the figure is cited via 365 Data Science and could not be directly re-verified.)*
- Survey data (Alteryx, via 365 Data Science): **70% of analysts say AI automation enhances their effectiveness**, and 87% feel more strategically valuable than ever. [27]

## Salary & job-market signals

- Entry-level data analyst salaries rose to ~**$90,000 in 2026**, up ~$20,000 year-over-year. [27]
- **Machine learning mentions in data-analyst job postings doubled** to 14% in 2026 — evidence that AI skills are now expected even in entry-level analytics roles. [27]

## The evolving role

Experts agree the analyst is **not being replaced — it is being reshaped**. The shift is:

- From **routine reporting** to **strategic interpretation and decision support**.
- From **building dashboards** to **interpreting AI-generated insights**, ensuring data quality, and telling data stories. [29] [30]
- A reported warning: as users query AI directly, analysts lose informal touchpoints with stakeholders and must deliberately maintain them. [30]
- AI literacy is expected to be "table stakes" by 2027 — like email or spreadsheets. [31]
