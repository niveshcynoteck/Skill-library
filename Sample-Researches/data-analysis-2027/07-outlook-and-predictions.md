*These are analyst forecasts (opinions), not certainties. Where possible, predictions are attributed and dated.*

## What these point to

1. **Dashboards give way to conversations and agents** — the interface to data becomes chat, not charts.
2. **Autonomy grows, but so does the need for governance** — guardian agents and human validation become standard.
3. **Model economics shift to small, task-specific models** — cheaper and more controllable than monolithic LLMs.
4. **Real-time and edge analytics expand** — 60%+ of enterprises on unified edge frameworks by 2027. [24]
5. **The winners are AI-literate organizations** — leadership education is framed as a financial-performance differentiator. [2]
