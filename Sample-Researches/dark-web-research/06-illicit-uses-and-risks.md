# Illicit Uses & Risks

The dark web is also widely used for illegal activity. Rough 2026 breakdowns of dark web content by category:

- **Illegal file sharing** (pirated media/software): ~29%
- **Leaked/stolen data** (credentials, databases, personal records): ~28%
- Drug marketplaces are estimated to move roughly 20% of global illicit drug sales.

## Key Risk Categories

- **Stolen credential markets** — billions of username/password pairs circulate; compromised credentials are a leading entry point for cyberattacks.
- **Scams** — exit scams (vendors vanishing with funds) and fake/clone marketplace sites are extremely common; the dark web is largely unregulated, so there's no consumer protection.
- **Malware** — unregulated sites carry elevated risk of drive-by malware.
- **Ransomware-as-a-Service and AI-enabled fraud** — an emerging and growing trend as of 2026.
