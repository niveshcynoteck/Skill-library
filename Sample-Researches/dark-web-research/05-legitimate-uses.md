# Legitimate Uses

- **Whistleblowing platforms**: Tools like **SecureDrop** and **GlobaLeaks** let sources submit documents to journalists anonymously. SecureDrop alone is used by 35+ major outlets, including The Guardian, The New York Times, The Washington Post, and The Intercept.
- **Journalism & press freedom**: Outlets such as the **BBC** and **New York Times** maintain official .onion mirrors so readers in censored regions can access uncensored news.
- **Activism & human rights**: Dissidents and human rights defenders in repressive regimes use dark web anonymity to organize, communicate, and report abuses without exposing themselves to state surveillance.
- **Privacy-conscious general use**: Some individuals use it simply to browse without tracking, in jurisdictions with heavy internet censorship.
