# Sources and Verification Notes

## Verification Notes

- Host city, format, draw, and final-score facts were corroborated across multiple sources (FIFA.com search results, ESPN, ABC News, NBC News, Al Jazeera, Wikipedia).
- One early web-search summary incorrectly implied Messi won a second Golden Ball; the Al Jazeera article (and other outlets) confirm **Rodri** won the Golden Ball, with Messi as runner-up — this write-up uses the corrected, corroborated version.
- The $15 billion revenue figure and the "nearly 7 million attendance" figure came from a single aggregated source (Wikipedia summary) and were not independently cross-checked against a second source — flagged as lower-confidence.
- Direct FIFA.com fetches returned empty/JS-rendered content in several attempts; facts relying solely on FIFA.com were cross-checked against other outlets where possible.
