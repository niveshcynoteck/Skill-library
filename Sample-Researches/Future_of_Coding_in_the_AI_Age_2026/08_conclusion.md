# Conclusion

The evidence supports a clear but non-catastrophic read: AI has taken over a large and growing share of the *mechanical* act of writing code — verified estimates range from ~30% to over 50% of code at major companies and platforms. What it has not done is eliminate the need for engineering judgment; if anything, it has made that judgment more concentrated and more valuable, while sharply devaluing the "translate ticket to code" work that used to be how junior engineers earned their seat.

The most credible near-term outlook isn't "coding is over," it's "the entry point to the profession is being rebuilt," with genuine disagreement even among AI lab CEOs about how fast and how far that goes — as shown by their own public reversal within months of the most extreme predictions.
