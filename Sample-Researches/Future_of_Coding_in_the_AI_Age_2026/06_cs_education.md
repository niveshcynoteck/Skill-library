# Computer Science Education Is Reorganizing Around This

Education is adapting in a directionally consistent way at every level:

- **K-12:** Code.org is rebranding to **CodeAI**, and its flagship "Hour of Code" event is becoming **"Hour of AI"** — teaching how the technology works rather than just how to write loops.

  Sources: [EdWeek](https://www.edweek.org/technology/momentum-builds-to-expand-coding-education-to-learning-about-ai-under-the-hood/2026/06), [EdWeek Market Brief](https://marketbrief.edweek.org/education-market/computer-science-education-will-look-different-in-the-age-of-ai-says-code-orgs-new-ceo/2026/03)

- **University:** Columbia and UC San Diego are both increasing emphasis on **code reading and review** over manual construction, with students now graded on assessing the quality of AI-generated code rather than just producing their own.

  Source: [UC San Diego Today — Transforming Computer Science Education in the Age of AI](https://today.ucsd.edu/story/transforming-computer-science-education-in-the-age-of-ai)

- **The stated direction industry-wide:** a shift "from manual coding to AI orchestration" — training systems architects and auditors rather than pure syntax-writers.
