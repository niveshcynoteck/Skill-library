# 4. Speed to Market and Scalability

- Outsourced teams can typically get an AI MVP live in **weeks to ~4 months**, versus **6–12 months** for an in-house team to reach first measurable value (accounting for hiring, ramp-up, and infrastructure setup). ([Groovy Web](https://www.groovyweb.co/blog/in-house-vs-outsource-ai-development-2026), [Colan Infotech](https://colaninfotech.com/blog/in-house-vs-outsourcing-ai-development-in-2026/))
- AI coding assistants (Copilot, Cursor, etc.) speed up the *coding* phase by 25–40% — but that doesn't touch discovery, design, or testing time, so it narrows the gap without eliminating it.
- Outsourced engagements flex up and down with need — useful for a big initial build followed by a lighter maintenance phase. In-house headcount, once hired, is a fixed cost regardless of workload.

## Trade-off

Speed and elasticity come at the cost of control — see `05-Risk-Factors.md` and `06-Control-Culture-and-Knowledge-Retention.md`.
