# In-House AI Team vs. Outsourcing to an AI Development Partner

A research report comparing two paths to building AI capability: hiring and running an **in-house AI team**, versus **outsourcing** to an external AI development partner (freelancer, dedicated team, agency, or managed AI service provider) — with a look at the **hybrid** model most enterprises are converging on in 2026.

Research conducted and compiled on **12 August 2026**. All references were validated for reachability; see `validated_links.csv`. Three sources could not be validated and were excluded — see the note at the end of `09-References.md`.
