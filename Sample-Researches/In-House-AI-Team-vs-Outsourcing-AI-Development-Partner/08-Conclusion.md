# 8. Conclusion

Neither model is categorically better — the right call depends on time horizon and how core the capability is:

- **Pick in-house** if the AI capability *is* your product, you can attract/retain the talent, and you're building for the long run.
- **Pick outsourcing** if you need to validate an idea fast, the need is bounded or non-core, and you're comfortable with elevated (but manageable, with the right contract) security/IP/lock-in risk.
- **Pick hybrid** — the direction most companies are converging on in 2026 — if the capability matters strategically but you don't yet have the bench to build it alone: keep governance and architecture decisions in-house, and let a partner carry execution load.
