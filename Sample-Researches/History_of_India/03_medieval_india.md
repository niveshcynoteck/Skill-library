# Medieval India (1206–1526)

## The Delhi Sultanate — Five Dynasties

- **Mamluk/Slave (1206–1290):** Founded by **Qutb-ud-din Aibak** (began the Qutb Minar); consolidated by **Iltutmish** (r. 1210–1236), who fended off Genghis Khan's Mongols; **Razia Sultana** (r. 1236–1240) — the only woman to rule the Sultanate, overthrown by Turkic nobles; **Ghiyas-ud-din Balban** (r. 1266–1287) repelled repeated Mongol incursions and centralized royal authority.
- **Khilji (1290–1320):** Founded by Jalal-ud-din Khilji. **Alauddin Khilji** (r. 1296–1316) expanded into the Deccan, defeated a Mongol force (1306), and imposed strict market-price controls (fixed prices, state granaries) to sustain his large standing army.
- **Tughlaq (1320–1413):** Founded by Ghiyasuddin Tughlaq. **Muhammad bin Tughlaq**'s disastrous capital shift to Daulatabad (1327) and failed token-currency experiment; **Firoz Shah Tughlaq** built canals and nearly 300 public works; Timur's sack of Delhi (1398) crippled the dynasty.
- **Sayyid (1414–1451):** A weak interregnum under Khizr Khan, installed as Timur's governor.
- **Lodi (1451–1526):** Founded by Bahlul Lodi. **Sikandar Lodi** (r. 1489–1517) founded Agra and reformed revenue administration; **Ibrahim Lodi** (r. 1517–1526) was defeated and killed by **Babur** at the **First Battle of Panipat (21 April 1526)**, ending the Sultanate and beginning Mughal rule.

Administration ran on the **iqta system** (non-hereditary revenue assignments to military commanders); architecture fused Indo-Islamic styles, epitomized by the Qutb Minar complex.

## The Vijayanagara Empire (1336–1646)

Founded in 1336 by brothers **Harihara I** and **Bukka Raya I** (Sangama dynasty), capital at Vijayanagara (modern Hampi). Peaked under **Krishnadeva Raya** (Tuluva dynasty, r. 1509–1529) — a warrior-poet fluent in multiple languages who defeated the Gajapatis of Odisha and the Bijapur Sultanate, and patronized Telugu literature (authored *Amuktamalyada*). Governed via the **Nayankara system** (military governors holding land grants in exchange for troops/revenue); thrived on Indian Ocean trade with Persia, Arabia, and Southeast Asia.

**Decline:** Under regent Rama Raya, a "Grand Alliance" of the Deccan sultanates (Bijapur, Ahmadnagar, Golconda, Bidar) defeated Vijayanagara at the **Battle of Talikota (23 January 1565)**; Rama Raya was captured and beheaded, and the capital was sacked. The empire survived nominally for another century under the Aravidu dynasty but never regained its power.

## Other Regional Powers

- **Bahmani Sultanate (1347–1527):** First independent Deccan Islamic sultanate, founded by Alauddin Hasan Bahman Shah after rebelling against Muhammad bin Tughlaq. Peaked under minister **Mahmud Gawan** before fragmenting into five Deccan Sultanates (notably Bijapur and Golconda).
- **Rajput Kingdoms:** **Prithviraj Chauhan** fought Muhammad Ghori at the Battles of Tarain (1191, 1192), losing the second and opening North India to Muslim rule. Mewar rose under **Rana Kumbha** (r. 1438–1468) and **Rana Sanga** (r. 1509–1527), who defeated Malwa, Gujarat, and Ibrahim Lodi's forces before losing to Babur at the **Battle of Khanwa (1527)**.
