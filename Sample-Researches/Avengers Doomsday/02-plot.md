# Plot

Marvel's official synopsis, as confirmed on Marvel.com and reiterated at San Diego Comic-Con 2026, states the film follows **"beloved heroes from three distinct universes"** as they are set on a "deadly collision course" and face "an existential threat unlike anything they've ever encountered." Beyond this, Marvel Studios has not released detailed plot specifics — this is explicitly a marketing choice, and any deeper "leaks" circulating online are unverified.

The "three universes" language refers to the crossover of:
1. The core **MCU** (Avengers characters)
2. The **Fantastic Four** universe (recently reintroduced via *The Fantastic Four: First Steps*)
3. The **Fox X-Men** universe (legacy characters from 20th Century Fox's X-Men films)
