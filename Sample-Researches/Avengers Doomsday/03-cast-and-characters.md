# Cast & Characters

## Central Villain
- **Robert Downey Jr.** as **Victor von Doom / Doctor Doom** — his high-profile return to the MCU, this time as the franchise's central antagonist rather than Iron Man.

## Returning MCU Heroes
- Chris Hemsworth (Thor)
- Chris Evans (Steve Rogers/Captain America)
- Anthony Mackie (Sam Wilson/Captain America)
- Sebastian Stan (Bucky Barnes)
- Paul Rudd (Ant-Man)
- Simu Liu (Shang-Chi)
- Letitia Wright (Shuri/Black Panther)
- Winston Duke (M'Baku)
- Tenoch Huerta Mejia (Namor)
- Danny Ramirez (Falcon)
- Tom Hiddleston (Loki)
- Wyatt Russell, Lewis Pullman, Kathryn Newton, and others
- **Hayley Atwell** confirmed to return as Peggy Carter

## Fantastic Four
- Pedro Pascal as Reed Richards/Mister Fantastic
- Vanessa Kirby as Sue Storm/Invisible Woman
- Joseph Quinn as Johnny Storm/Human Torch
- Ebon Moss-Bachrach as Ben Grimm/The Thing

## X-Men (Legacy Fox Universe)
- Patrick Stewart as Professor Charles Xavier
- Ian McKellen (reportedly reprising Magneto)
- Rebecca Romijn as Mystique
- Alan Cumming as Nightcrawler
- James Marsden and Kelsey Grammer (Beast) also among the announced legacy X-Men cast

## Other Notable Additions
- Channing Tatum, David Harbour, Mabel Cadena, Hannah John-Kamen

*Note: Cast lists are drawn from Marvel's own announcements plus Comic-Con reveals; some names circulating in entertainment press (e.g., full character assignments for every actor) haven't been individually confirmed by Marvel Studios and should be treated as reported rather than officially verified in every case.*
