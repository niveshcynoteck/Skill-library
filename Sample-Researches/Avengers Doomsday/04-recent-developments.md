# Recent Developments (San Diego Comic-Con 2026)

At SDCC 2026's Hall H panel, Marvel Studios:
- Officially confirmed release date and extensive cast for *Doomsday*
- Screened an exclusive in-room trailer/footage preview for attendees
- Distributed Doctor Doom masks/cloaks to the audience, with Robert Downey Jr. leading an interactive moment
- Also revealed a new **Ghost Rider** film starring Ryan Gosling (directed by Shawn Levy, slated for 2028) and teased **Black Panther 3**
