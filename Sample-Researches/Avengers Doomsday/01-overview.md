# Overview

**Avengers: Doomsday** is an upcoming Marvel Studios superhero film and the fifth installment in the *Avengers* franchise, serving as the direct lead-in to *Avengers: Secret Wars*. It marks the beginning of the Marvel Cinematic Universe's "Multiverse Saga" climax.

| Detail | Information |
|---|---|
| **Release date** | December 18, 2026 (theatrical) |
| **Directors** | Anthony Russo & Joe Russo |
| **Producers** | Kevin Feige, Louis D'Esposito, Jonathan Schwartz |
| **Studio** | Marvel Studios / Walt Disney Studios |
| **Filming** | Began April 2025 in London; principal photography has wrapped, with additional photography expected |
| **Predecessor** | Sets up *Avengers: Secret Wars* (also Russo-directed) |
