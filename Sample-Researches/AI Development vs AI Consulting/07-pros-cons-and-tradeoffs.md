# 7. Pros, Cons & Trade-offs

| Factor | AI Developer / Engineer | AI Consultant |
|---|---|---|
| **Stability** | High — salary, benefits, employment contract | Low–medium — income varies, must find clients |
| **Earnings ceiling** | Capped by salary band unless Staff/Principal | Effectively uncapped (elite rates, retainers, leverage) |
| **Learning/mentorship** | Strong — structured teams, senior mentors | Self-directed — you must build your own practice |
| **Flexibility** | Lower — fixed schedule/projects | High — pick clients, hours, location |
| **Variety of work** | Deeper in one product/system | Across industries, problems, and teams |
| **Business risk** | Minimal — company-level risk only | High — self-employment tax, health costs, feast-or-famine |
| **Work-life balance** | Generally predictable; can spike during deployments | Flexible but blurry; new consultants often undercharge and overwork |
| **Need to sell** | None (internal) | Constant — sales, marketing, scoping, pricing |

Sources: [Zen van Riel](https://zenvanriel.com/job/ai-engineer-vs-ai-consultant/), [Research.com](https://research.com/advice/which-artificial-intelligence-degree-careers-offer-the-best-work-life-balance), [Let's Data Science](https://letsdatascience.com/blog/go-freelance-or-stay-employed-the-real-math-behind-ai-consulting-in-2026)

Key observations on balance: **42% of AI professionals who moved into consulting or educational roles report significantly better work-life balance** than in research/engineering roles — but that reflects independence, not guaranteed outcomes [Research.com](https://research.com/advice/which-artificial-intelligence-degree-careers-offer-the-best-work-life-balance). Consultants typically bill 20–30 hrs/week and spend another 10–15 on sales/admin/learning — roughly a full-time equivalent, just self-managed [Zen van Riel](https://zenvanriel.com/job/ai-engineer-vs-ai-consultant/).
