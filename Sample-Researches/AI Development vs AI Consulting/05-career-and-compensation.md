## The "employed vs independent" financial math

This is a crucial, frequently-misunderstood point:

- A consultant at **$1,000/day × 180 billable days ≈ $180,000 gross**. That *gross* figure roughly equals what a **senior ML engineer earns as net employee compensation** — but the employee gets health insurance, 401(k) match, paid vacation, and zero business-development overhead. [Let's Data Science](https://letsdatascience.com/blog/go-freelance-or-stay-employed-the-real-math-behind-ai-consulting-in-2026)
- **To break even with a $180K employed package, an independent needs ≈ $250K gross** — because of self-employment tax (~15%), self-funded health insurance, tools, downtime, and unpaid vacation. [Let's Data Science](https://letsdatascience.com/blog/go-freelance-or-stay-employed-the-real-math-behind-ai-consulting-in-2026)
- Full-time benefits at top tech companies are worth **$30K–$80K/year** [Zen van Riel](https://zenvanriel.com/job/ai-engineer-vs-ai-consultant/)
- Year-one freelance revenue for most new consultants is **$60K–$120K**; stability typically arrives around month 12–18 as referrals activate. [Let's Data Science](https://letsdatascience.com/blog/go-freelance-or-stay-employed-the-real-math-behind-ai-consulting-in-2026)

**Bottom line:** a strong independent consultant can out-earn an employee, but the *average* independent earns similarly to an employed engineer with more stress and variability. The real upside is at senior/elite levels ($400–$700/hr). [Zen van Riel](https://zenvanriel.com/job/ai-engineer-vs-ai-consultant/)

## Market & demand outlook

Both fields are growing strongly:

- **BLS:** computer and information research scientists projected **+20% growth 2024–2034** (median pay $140,910); data scientists **+33.5%**; software developers **+15.8%** — all "much faster than average." [BLS OOH](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/computer-and-information-technology/computer-and-information-research-scientists.htm), [BLS TED](https://www.bls.gov/opub/ted/2026/artificial-intelligence-information-technology-and-employment-2024-34.htm)
- **WEF Future of Jobs Report 2025:** AI & machine learning specialists are among the **fastest-growing job roles by 2030**; AI & information-processing tech is expected to create ~11 million jobs. [WEF](https://www.weforum.org/publications/the-future-of-jobs-report-2025/digest/)
- **AI consulting market:** estimated **$8.96B in 2026**, growing to **$19.47B by 2030** (~21% CAGR) [Research and Markets](https://www.researchandmarkets.com/reports/6245023/artificial-intelligence-ai-consulting-market); other analyses estimate **$14B in 2026 → $73–116B by 2033–35** (26–27% CAGR) [Persistence Market Research](https://www.persistencemarketresearch.com/market-research/ai-consulting-services-market.asp), [AIDOLS](https://aidolsgroup.com/en/blog/category/research-report/ai-consulting-statistics-2026/)
