# 8. How They Overlap & How to Choose

## The overlap is growing

The market is moving **away from "pure strategy consultant vs pure developer"** toward hybrid roles that combine strategic thinking with hands-on execution [Dan Cumberland Labs](https://dancumberlandlabs.com/blog/ai-consultant-vs-developer/). For small and mid-market firms, the cleanest setup is usually **one person who can both consult and build** — paying for two separate people means paying for context to be transferred twice [Vlad Brakalo](https://vladbrakalo.com/blog/ai-consultant-vs-ai-engineer/).

## The correct sequence (for most projects)

> **Consult → Plan → Build → Improve**

Skipping the consulting phase leads to wrong use cases, budget overruns, and low adoption [Rubixe](https://rubixe.com/blog/ai-consulting-vs-ai-development).

## Decision framework — which gap is bigger right now?

| Your situation | What you need |
|---|---|
| You have a budget for "AI" but no specific problem | **Consultant first** (diagnosis, roadmap, build-vs-buy) |
| 3–4 competing ideas, need them ranked | **Consultant** (value/effort scoring) |
| You already failed once (unused chatbot, broken automation) | **Consultant** (second opinion before spending more) |
| You know exactly what to build (clear inputs/outputs) | **Engineer/developer** (implementation) |
| A prototype works but won't scale / can't integrate | **Engineer** (productionize it) |
| You're building a product customers depend on | **Engineer / in-house team** |

Source: [Vlad Brakalo](https://vladbrakalo.com/blog/ai-consultant-vs-ai-engineer/), [Aatvi AI](https://aatvi.ai/en/insights/ai-consulting-vs-ai-software-development)

## Career-fit checklist (for individuals choosing a path)

Choose **AI development** if you:
- Enjoy deep technical execution — building, shipping, debugging systems
- Prefer stability, mentorship, and structured growth
- Like working within teams on one product over time
- Don't want to sell or manage clients

Choose **AI consulting** if you:
- Enjoy variety, client-facing work, and business context
- Are comfortable with sales, pricing, and business development
- Have 5+ years experience and a strong network
- Accept income variability for autonomy and uncapped upside
- Are willing to specialize in a vertical (healthcare, fintech, legal) — this is the strongest hedge as generic AI skills commoditize [MentorCruise](https://mentorcruise.com/blog/how-to-become-an-ai-consultant/)

The most common safe transition path: **build expertise as an employee first (1–5 years), then transition to consulting** once you have reputation, a portfolio, and savings (6–12 months of expenses). [Zen van Riel](https://zenvanriel.com/job/ai-engineer-vs-ai-consultant/)
