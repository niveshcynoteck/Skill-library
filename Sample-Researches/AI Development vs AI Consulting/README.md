# AI Development vs AI Consulting

**Date:** August 2026
**Type:** Research report
**Visual:** Comparison diagram (embedded Mermaid)

## Overview

This research compares two complementary ways of working with AI:

- **AI Development** — the execution phase: building, integrating, testing, and deploying working AI-powered software.
- **AI Consulting** — the strategy and advisory phase: diagnosing which problems are worth solving with AI, designing roadmaps, selecting vendors, and managing change.

> An AI consultant tells you what to build, in what order, and with what trade-offs. An AI engineer writes the code that does the thing. Some people do both, but the daily work is quite different. — [Vlad Brakalo](https://vladbrakalo.com/blog/ai-consultant-vs-ai-engineer/)

## Key Conclusions

- **AI development and AI consulting are complementary, not competing.** The developer builds; the consultant decides what's worth building and why.
- Most real projects need both, **in sequence** (consult → plan → build → improve) — and increasingly from the same person.
- **Choose development** when you love building and want stability.
- **Choose consulting** when you love decisions, people, and variety, and can tolerate income variability.
- Both are among the **fastest-growing career fields** of this decade, and both reward seniority, specialization, and demonstrable production experience.
