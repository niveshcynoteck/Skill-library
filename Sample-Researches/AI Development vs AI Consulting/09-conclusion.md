# 9. Conclusion

**AI development and AI consulting are complementary, not competing.** The developer builds; the consultant decides what's worth building and why. Most real projects need both, in sequence — and increasingly from the same person.

- Choose **development** when you love building and want stability.
- Choose **consulting** when you love decisions, people, and variety, and can tolerate income variability.
- Both are among the fastest-growing career fields of this decade, and both reward seniority, specialization, and demonstrable production experience over generic AI literacy.

## The correct sequence (recap)

> **Consult → Plan → Build → Improve**
