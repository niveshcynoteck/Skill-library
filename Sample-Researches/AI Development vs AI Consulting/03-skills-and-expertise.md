# 3. Skills & Expertise Required

## AI Developer / Engineer — the technical stack

- **Programming:** Python dominates; also Java, C++, TypeScript for frontend-adjacent work [IBM](https://www.ibm.com/think/topics/ai-developer)
- **ML frameworks:** PyTorch, TensorFlow, Keras, Scikit-learn, Hugging Face Transformers [Wiz Academy](https://www.wiz.io/academy/cloud-careers/ai-developer)
- **Data work:** Pandas, NumPy, SQL, ETL, feature engineering — developers spend roughly **60–70% of their time** on data preparation and integration [Wiz Academy](https://www.wiz.io/academy/cloud-careers/ai-developer)
- **Applied LLM skills:** prompt/context design, RAG pipelines (chunking, embedding, retrieval, reranking), tool-calling, structured output, evaluation of non-deterministic output [CTAIO](https://ctaio.dev/en/ai-careers/ai-developer/)
- **Production/MLOps:** Docker, Kubernetes, CI/CD, cloud services (AWS Bedrock, GCP Vertex AI, Azure ML), observability (LangSmith, LangFuse, Prometheus), monitoring for drift and cost [Hyperskill](https://hyperskill.org/learn/step/53489)
- **Soft skills:** analytical problem-solving, systems thinking, debugging probabilistic failures, communication with non-technical stakeholders [Wiz Academy](https://www.wiz.io/academy/cloud-careers/ai-developer)

## AI Consultant — the hybrid toolkit

Consultants need **five technical + five advisory skills** [Advisori](https://www.advisori.de/en/blog/how-to-become-ai-consultant-career-path-skills-certifications):

| Technical foundation | Advisory / soft skills |
|---|---|
| ML foundations (understand feasibility, not build models) | Use-case mapping & prioritization |
| MLOps & productionization concepts | Change management (AI projects fail on people, not models) |
| LLMs & GenAI (prompting, RAG, hallucination evaluation) | ROI modeling & business-case building |
| Data engineering basics (data quality, ETL, governance) | Stakeholder translation (CFO/IT/board speak different languages) |
| Regulatory literacy (EU AI Act, GDPR, ISO 42001) | Executive communication (one page, three bullets, one decision) |

Consultants also need **enough engineering to call out bad vendor pitches**, while engineers need **enough business context to not build the wrong thing** — the middle of the Venn diagram is real but rare [Vlad Brakalo](https://vladbrakalo.com/blog/ai-consultant-vs-ai-engineer/).
