# 6. Business Models & Clients

## AI Development — product-oriented

- **Where they work:** tech companies, startups, SaaS, in-house enterprise teams, research labs [Qoollege](https://careers.qoollege.com/compare/artificial-intelligence-consultant-vs-artificial-intelligence-engineer)
- **Revenue model:** salary + equity, or billing as a contractor/agency for defined builds
- **Client relationship:** part of a team or agency; ongoing as the product evolves
- **What buyers expect:** working software, reliability, scalability [Vlad Brakalo](https://vladbrakalo.com/blog/ai-consultant-vs-ai-engineer/)

## AI Consulting — advisory & services

- **Where they work:** consulting firms (Deloitte, Accenture, McKinsey, boutiques), independent practice, fractional/part-time advisory [Future Skills Academy](https://futureskillsacademy.com/blog/ai-analyst-vs-ai-engineer-vs-ai-consultant-roles/)
- **Revenue models:**
  - Hourly/day rates ($150–$1,000+/hr by firm tier)
  - Fixed project fees (assessments $25K–$75K, POCs $50K–$250K, single use case $100K–$500K, enterprise programs $500K–$5M+)
  - Monthly retainers ($10K–$100K/month for ongoing advisory) [AIDOLS](https://aidolsgroup.com/en/blog/category/research-report/ai-consulting-cost-guide/)
  - Increasingly **outcome-based pricing** tied to measurable KPIs [AIDOLS](https://aidolsgroup.com/en/blog/category/industry-insights/state-of-ai-consulting-2026/)
- **Client relationship:** engagement ends when the deliverable is done; best consultants work themselves out of a job by training the client team [OpenKit](https://openkit.co.uk/blog/posts/what-does-an-ai-consultant-do)

## A 2026 trend worth knowing

The AI consulting market is shifting from **advisory-heavy** engagements toward **engineering-first** delivery: fixed-fee, 90-day sprints that ship production systems, driven by high AI project failure rates and buyers demanding outcome accountability. [AIDOLS](https://aidolsgroup.com/en/blog/category/industry-insights/state-of-ai-consulting-2026/)
