# 1. Definitions & Core Concepts

Both terms are used loosely in the market, and job titles overlap. This report treats them as two distinct activities that often combine in one person or one engagement.

## What is AI Development?

AI development is the **execution phase** — building, integrating, testing, and deploying working AI-powered software. An AI developer (or AI engineer) turns a defined requirement into a running system.

- **Primary question:** *How do we build and ship it?*
- **Main output:** production code, deployed systems, pipelines, models
- **Typical deliverable:** working software that runs in production
- **Example:** building a chatbot that answers customer queries by pulling from a company's internal knowledge base (RAG), or an email-routing system that classifies and auto-replies.

Sources: [Rubixe](https://rubixe.com/blog/ai-consulting-vs-ai-development), [IBM](https://www.ibm.com/think/topics/ai-developer), [zalt.me](https://zalt.me/blog/2026/05/what-does-ai-consultant-do)

A useful internal distinction: an **AI developer** works at the *application layer* (wiring models into products via APIs, prompts, retrieval), while an **AI engineer** works *closer to the model* (pipelines, evaluation infrastructure, inference, serving). In practice, employers use the titles interchangeably. [CTAIO](https://ctaio.dev/en/ai-careers/ai-developer/)

## What is AI Consulting?

AI consulting is the **strategy and advisory phase** — diagnosing which problems are worth solving with AI, designing the roadmap, selecting technology/vendors, managing change, and often overseeing implementation.

- **Primary question:** *Should we do this, and how?*
- **Main output:** decisions, strategy, roadmaps, feasibility analyses
- **Typical deliverable:** a plan, decision document, or program roadmap (a consultant may or may not write production code)
- **Example:** auditing a business's workflows, ranking AI use cases by value and effort, and recommending whether to build, buy, or use features already in the tools the company pays for.

Sources: [Vlad Brakalo](https://vladbrakalo.com/blog/ai-consultant-vs-ai-engineer/), [CIO](https://www.cio.com/article/3951929/what-does-an-ai-consultant-actually-do.html), [AIMindShift](https://aimindshift.consulting/guides/ai-consultant-vs-ai-engineer/)

## The simplest summary

> An **AI consultant tells you what to build, in what order, and with what trade-offs. An AI engineer writes the code that does the thing.** Some people do both, but the daily work is quite different. [Vlad Brakalo](https://vladbrakalo.com/blog/ai-consultant-vs-ai-engineer/)
