# Overview

The Monsoon Session of the Indian Parliament (2026) is one of the three regular sessions held each year — alongside the Budget Session (February–April) and the Winter Session (November–December). It is convened under **Article 85 of the Constitution**, which requires that the gap between two parliamentary sessions not exceed six months.

| Detail | Information |
|---|---|
| **Session dates** | 20 July 2026 – 13 August 2026 |
| **Duration** | Nearly four weeks |
| **Number of sittings** | 19 |
| **Pending bills** | 28 |
| **Pre-session ritual** | All-party meeting convened by the Speaker (a customary step ahead of every session) |
