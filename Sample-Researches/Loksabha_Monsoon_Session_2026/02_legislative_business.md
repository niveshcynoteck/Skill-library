# Key Legislative Business

The government has prioritized **seven bills** out of the 28 pending, covering economic governance, judicial capacity, education regulation, and compliance reform.

## Bills for introduction, consideration, and passing

| Bill | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Income-tax (Amendment) Bill, 2026** | Replaces the Income-tax (Amendment) Ordinance, 2026; exempts certain foreign institutional investors and the Bank for International Settlements from income tax on specified earnings from government securities |
| **Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises Development (Amendment) Bill, 2026** | Strengthens mechanisms against delayed payments to MSMEs, enforcement of arbitral awards, and gives states greater authority over facilitation councils |
| **Registration of Births and Deaths (Amendment) Bill, 2026** | Tightens provisions around delayed registration |
| **Supreme Court (Number of Judges) Amendment Bill, 2026** | Increases the sanctioned strength of Supreme Court judges (excluding the CJI) from **33 to 37**, to enhance judicial capacity |
| **Prevention of Insults to National Honour (Amendment) Bill, 2026** | Amends the original 1971 Act |

## Bills for consideration and passing

| Bill | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Foreign Contribution (Regulation) Amendment Bill, 2026** | Establishes a framework for supervising, managing, and disposing of foreign contributions/assets of organizations whose FCRA registration has lapsed; also reduces the maximum imprisonment penalty from five years to one year |
| **Viksit Bharat Shiksha Adhishthan Bill, 2025** | Replaces the UGC, AICTE, and NCTE with a single unified higher-education regulator |
