# Major Political Flashpoints

## 1. Joint Parliamentary Committee (JPC) report on the 130th Constitution Amendment Bill

- The JPC met on **17 July 2026** to adopt its findings before submission to Parliament.
- Sources indicate the committee is expected to **retain the Bill's most contested provision** — automatic removal of a Prime Minister, Chief Minister, or minister from office after **30 consecutive days in judicial custody** for serious offences — while adding safeguards against political misuse.
- This remains highly contentious: supporters frame it as strengthening accountability, while critics warn it could be misused to destabilize elected governments through politically motivated prosecutions.

## 2. "Operation Sindoor" row

- The Opposition (led by Congress and allies) is reportedly planning **privilege proceedings against Defence Minister Rajnath Singh**, alleging he gave misleading statements in Parliament about Indian soldier casualties during Operation Sindoor.
- This is expected to be a major point of confrontation between the treasury and opposition benches during the session.
