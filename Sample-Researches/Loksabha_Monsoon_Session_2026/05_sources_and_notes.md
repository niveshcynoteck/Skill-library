# Sources and Verification Notes

## Note on Verification

The dates, bill list, and sitting count are corroborated across multiple sources (PRS India's official Session Alert tracker, SCC Online, and Open magazine), giving reasonably high confidence. However, some search results returned mixed references to older articles about past Monsoon Sessions (e.g., 2024, 2025, and even much older sessions) — these were filtered out and not used in this summary. Details on the *final* content/passage status of specific bills (e.g., whether the Supreme Court judges bill actually passed) could not be confirmed since the session was ongoing/very recent relative to available data — treat any outcome-related claims for bills as pending unless explicitly confirmed post-session.
