# 11. Established Facts and Open Questions

## Strongly Supported

- Humans are primates and great apes.
- Chimpanzees and bonobos are our closest living relatives.
- Humans and living apes share extinct common ancestors.
- Bipedalism preceded major brain expansion.
- Several hominin species coexisted.
- *Homo sapiens* originated in Africa.
- Neanderthals, Denisovans and *Homo sapiens* interbred.
- All living humans belong to one species, *Homo sapiens*.

## Still Uncertain

- Which early hominin species were direct ancestors
- The exact population that gave rise to the genus *Homo*
- Whether *Homo habilis* was directly ancestral to *Homo erectus*
- How Middle Pleistocene populations should be classified
- The exact African populations and locations involved in our origin
- Precisely when fully developed language emerged
- When controlled fire became habitual
- The number and locations of interbreeding events
- Why Neanderthals disappeared as a distinct population
- How Denisovan fossils should be classified taxonomically
