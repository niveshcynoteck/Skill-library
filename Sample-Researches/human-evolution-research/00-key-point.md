# Key Point

Human evolution was **not a straight line** from an ape to a modern human. It was a branching history involving many populations and species. Several kinds of humans lived at the same time, and most branches eventually disappeared.

Living humans, *Homo sapiens*, are the only surviving human species. However, many people retain small amounts of DNA inherited through interbreeding with Neanderthals and Denisovans.
