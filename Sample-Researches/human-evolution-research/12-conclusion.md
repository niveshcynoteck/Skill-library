# Conclusion

The safest scientific description of the path to humans today is:

1. Humans and chimpanzees inherited traits from a shared African ancestor.
2. Early hominins developed forms of upright movement.
3. Australopith populations became diverse across Africa.
4. One still-unresolved branch produced the genus *Homo*.
5. Some African *Homo erectus*-related populations contributed to later humans.
6. Interacting African populations gradually developed the characteristics associated with *Homo sapiens*.
7. *Homo sapiens* expanded around the world and interbred with other human groups.
8. All people alive today belong to the same surviving human species.

Human evolution is best understood as a **branching network of populations**, with occasional reconnection through interbreeding, rather than a straight ladder leading inevitably to us.
