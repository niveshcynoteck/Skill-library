# 6. Origin of *Homo sapiens*

Fossil and genetic evidence strongly supports an **African origin** for *Homo sapiens*.

Jebel Irhoud in Morocco has early *Homo sapiens* fossils dated to approximately 315,000-300,000 years ago. They have a relatively modern face but a more elongated braincase.

Omo I in Ethiopia is older than approximately 233,000 years and displays a more recognizably modern skull form.

Early fossils occur across northern, eastern and southern Africa. A leading explanation is therefore **structured African evolution**: partly separated populations periodically exchanged genes and cultural innovations.

- Africa is strongly supported as the continent of origin.
- A precise single birthplace has not been established.
- There was probably no exact instant when an entirely modern species appeared.
- Modern human anatomy accumulated gradually across interacting populations.
