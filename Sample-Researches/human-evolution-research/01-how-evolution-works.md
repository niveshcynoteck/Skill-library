# 1. How Evolution Works

Evolution is a change in the inherited characteristics of a population over generations.

Its main mechanisms are:

| Mechanism | Simple explanation |
|---|---|
| Mutation | Produces new genetic variations |
| Recombination | Mixes parental DNA during reproduction |
| Natural selection | Helpful inherited traits may become more common |
| Genetic drift | Traits change in frequency by chance, especially in small populations |
| Gene flow | Populations exchange genes through migration and reproduction |

Evolution does not plan for the future or work toward perfection. Modern humans were not an inevitable destination.

Human traits also evolved at different times. Walking on two legs appeared millions of years before the large brains associated with later members of the genus *Homo*. This is called **mosaic evolution**.
