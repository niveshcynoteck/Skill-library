# 4. Important Side Branches

Many hominins were relatives rather than direct ancestors.

| Branch | Significance |
|---|---|
| *Paranthropus* | Heavily built chewing specialists that lived alongside early *Homo*; considered a side branch |
| *Homo naledi* | Small-brained South African hominin that survived surprisingly late |
| *Homo floresiensis* | Small-bodied island population from Flores, Indonesia |
| *Homo luzonensis* | Distinctive island hominin found in the Philippines |
| Neanderthals | Eurasian sister lineage that interbred with *Homo sapiens* |
| Denisovans | Asian sister lineage known primarily through DNA and a limited fossil record |

The existence of these branches shows why the traditional "march of progress" image is misleading.
