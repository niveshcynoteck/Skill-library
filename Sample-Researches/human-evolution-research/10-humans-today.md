# 10. What Makes Humans Today Different?

Living humans are not necessarily "more evolved" than other species. Every living species has been evolving since its ancestors separated from other lineages.

Important characteristics of living humans include:

- Extremely flexible behavior
- Complex language
- Extensive cumulative culture
- Large cooperative networks
- Long childhood and prolonged learning
- Sophisticated symbolic communication
- Technology that changes environments globally

Human biological evolution has not stopped. Comparatively recent adaptations include adult lactose digestion in some populations, high-altitude adaptations, infectious-disease resistance and variation associated with diet and climate.

Culture now changes much faster than genes, but biological and cultural evolution continue to influence each other.
