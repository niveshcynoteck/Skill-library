# 9. How Scientists Know

Human evolution is reconstructed by combining independent types of evidence.

| Method | What it reveals | Main limitation |
|---|---|---|
| Fossil anatomy | Body form, movement, growth and relationships | Similar traits may evolve independently |
| Archaeology | Tools, food processing, fire and behavior | Artifacts cannot always be assigned to one species |
| Ancient DNA | Ancestry, interbreeding and biological relationships | Preserves poorly in warm environments |
| Ancient proteins | Broad relationships where DNA is unavailable | Provides less detail than nuclear DNA |
| Sediment DNA | Presence of populations without identifiable bones | May move between layers |
| Radiocarbon dating | Dates relatively recent organic material | Effective only to roughly 50 ka |
| Uranium-series and ESR | Dates teeth, bones and cave deposits | Depends on environmental history |
| Luminescence dating | When sediment was last exposed to light | Disturbed sediment can mislead |
| Stratigraphy | Relative order of layers and finds | Burrowing or erosion may disturb layers |
| Genetic modelling | Population splits and admixture | Depends on mutation rates and demographic assumptions |

Scientific conclusions are strongest when several independent methods agree.
