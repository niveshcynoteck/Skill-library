# Human Evolution: The Path to Humans Today

This research explains human evolution as a branching population history rather than a straight ladder. It follows the cautious scientific path from the human-chimpanzee common ancestor through early hominins, australopiths and *Homo* populations to living *Homo sapiens*.

## Dating notation

- `Ma`: million years ago
- `ka`: thousand years ago

Dates are approximate. A fossil's age and anatomy do not prove that it belonged to a population directly ancestral to living humans.
