# 2. Humans and Other Primates

Humans belong to the primate order and the great-ape family.

Our closest living relatives are chimpanzees and bonobos. Both are equally related to us because chimpanzees and bonobos separated from each other after their shared lineage had already separated from ours.

Humans did **not** evolve from living chimpanzees or monkeys. Humans and chimpanzees instead share an extinct common ancestor that probably lived in Africa roughly 7-6 million years ago, although estimates vary.

```text
Earlier primates
       |
       +-- Orangutan lineage
       |
       +-- Gorilla lineage
       |
       +-- Common ancestor of humans, chimpanzees and bonobos
                  |
                  +-- Human lineage
                  |
                  +-- Chimpanzee and bonobo lineage
```

The term **hominin** refers to humans and fossil populations more closely related to humans than to chimpanzees after the lineages separated.
