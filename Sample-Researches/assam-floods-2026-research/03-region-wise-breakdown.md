# 3. Region-wise Breakdown of Assam

Assam is split into two broad valley regions — the **Brahmaputra Valley** (Lower, Central and Upper Assam) and the **Barak Valley** (southern districts). In 2026, the flooding was **concentrated in Upper Assam**, with a fresh wave building in the Lower Brahmaputra and Barak Valley in early August.

## 3.1 Upper Assam (epicentre of 2026 floods)

Districts: Sivasagar, Charaideo, Jorhat, Golaghat, Majuli, Dibrugarh, Tinsukia, Lakhimpur, Dhemaji.

| District | People affected (peak, 24 Jul) | Deaths | Notes |
|----------|-------------------------------|--------|-------|
| **Sivasagar** | ~60,000 | **42** | Worst hit. Dikhow river record breach flooded Sivasagar town areas; CM announced special relief package |
| **Charaideo** | ~31,000 | ~10 | Heavy damage around Sonari; large-scale evacuations |
| **Jorhat** | ~20,000 | ~7 | Dikhow catchment; several areas cut off, stranded people airlifted |
| **Golaghat** | ~8,000 | ~2 | Dhansiri river flooding |
| **Majuli** | — | — | River island severely threatened; erosion plus flood; riverbank breaching |
| **Lakhimpur / Dhemaji** | ~9,000 | — | Brahmaputra north-bank districts |

**Cumulative deaths in the Upper Assam region: ~70 of the 89 state-wide.**

## 3.2 Central and Lower Brahmaputra Valley (early-August wave)

Districts: Nagaon, Morigaon, Kamrup (Metro/Rural), Nalbari, Barpeta, Bongaigaon, Goalpara, Dhubri, Kokrajhar, South Salmara-Mankachar, Chirang.

- Entered the affected list in the first week of August as the Brahmaputra and its tributaries rose.
- **Dhubri, South Salmara-Mankachar, Kokrajhar and Kamrup** recorded fresh flooding (Brahmaputra + Sankosh + Jinjiram).
- Mostly riverine/embankment-breach flooding; evacuation started for char (river-island) dwellers.
- Deaths in this zone remained low relative to Upper Assam.

## 3.3 Barak Valley (southern districts)

Districts: Cachar, Karimganj, Hailakandi.

- **Cachar and Hailakandi** saw renewed flooding in early August (Barak river + tributaries).
- Historically the second-most flood-prone region after Upper Assam; localised in 2026 so far.

## 3.4 Hill districts and non-flood areas

- **Karbi Anglong, Dima Hasao, West Karbi Anglong:** flash floods/landslides risk (Dima Hasao rail line disruption was a major issue in past seasons); largely dry in 2026.
- **Urban flooding:** parts of Guwahati (Kamrup Metro) saw waterlogging, but not the widespread rural inundation of Upper Assam.
