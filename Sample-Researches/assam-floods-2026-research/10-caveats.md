# 10. Important Caveats

## 10.1 Data limitations

- **Figures change daily.** Death tolls and affected-person counts are point-in-time; the 89 figure is "as of 5 August 2026" and will likely rise.
- **The 7.28 lakh figure is a seasonal cumulative count** since 29 April, not a single-day number. Some outlets misreported it as a daily peak — treat large numbers with care.
- **Affected-person counts** include both camp residents and people marooned in homes; different agencies may count differently.

## 10.2 Claims that could not be independently verified

- Specific village-level casualty lists circulated on social media.
- Estimates of total financial damage (₹ figures vary across reports; the state's final assessment is pending).
- Some reports of a "total population affected across all districts" that mix daily and cumulative numbers.

## 10.3 Source reliability notes

- **Preferred sources:** ASDMA bulletins, state government releases, PTI/Business Standard/The Hindu/Indian Express (national wire), and regionally vetted outlets (NE Now, EastMojo, Shillong Times, The Sentinel).
- **One reference was dropped** during validation (NDTV Profit returned HTTP 403) and its claims were cross-verified against other sources.
- **Social media (X/WhatsApp):** casualty numbers, "no relief being given" claims, and attack allegations circulate unverified — do not act on them; report to helplines instead.

## 10.4 Safety of the reader

This is a research summary, **not** official disaster instructions. Always follow the ASDMA/NDRF and district administration instructions, which take precedence in an emergency.
