# Assam Floods 2026: Situation, Causes, and What to Do Now

*Last updated: 5 August 2026. Figures change daily; this report uses the latest officially reported data available at the time of writing.*

## Overview

This research covers the 2026 Assam floods: the current situation, a region-wise breakdown of the state, causes, government response and relief operations, practical advice on what to do now, health and safety guidance, how to help, and historical context.

## How to read the numbers

- **Lakh** = 100,000 (e.g., 1.22 lakh = 122,000 people).
- Figures are point-in-time daily counts from ASDMA bulletins unless marked "seasonal cumulative".
- The 22 July figure (7.28 lakh) is a seasonal cumulative count since 29 April, not comparable directly with daily counts.
