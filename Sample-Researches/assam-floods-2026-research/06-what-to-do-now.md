# 6. What to Do Now — Immediate Action

## 6.1 If you are in a flood-affected area

1. **Call the helplines first** — do not attempt rescue yourself.
   - National emergency: **112**
   - State disaster: **1070** | Flood helpline: **1079**
   - District control room: **1077**
   - ASDMA State Control Room: **0361-2237219 / 9401044617**
   - Email: **sdma-assam@gov.in**
2. **Move to the nearest relief camp / relief distribution centre** — carry documents (Aadhaar, land records, bank passbook) in a waterproof bag.
3. **Do not walk or drive through flowing water** — 15 cm of fast water can knock you over; 60 cm can carry away most vehicles.
4. **Turn off gas and electricity** at the mains before leaving; do not touch switches with wet hands.
5. **Watch official channels** (ASDMA, All India Radio, DD Assam) for bulletins — disregard unverified social-media flood warnings.
6. **Charge your phone** and keep a power bank; save the helpline numbers now.
7. **If your house is flooded**, mark water level, take photos for the damage claim, and register for assistance at the camp.

## 6.2 If you are outside the flood zone

- **Donate to verified relief funds** (see Section 8) rather than unsolicited in-kind goods — unsorted clothes/plastic often clog logistics.
- **Do not block rescue routes** with personal vehicles.
- **Volunteer** only through recognised organisations; untrained individuals are a liability.

## 6.3 Preparing for the second wave

- The season continues into September. **Lower Brahmaputra and Barak Valley** were rising in early August.
- Keep **emergency kits** ready: documents, water-purification tablets, ORS, first-aid, torch, matches, dry food, and a charged power bank.
- **Know your district helpline (1077)** and the nearest high ground / camp location in advance.
- **Livestock:** move animals to elevated ground early; stock fodder; report animal deaths to veterinary department.

## 6.4 Claims and paperwork

- **Death ex gratia (₹9 lakh):** applies for deaths caused directly by the flood; register with the relief camp/DC office.
- **Interim aid (₹15,000):** distributed via camps; keep Aadhaar and bank details ready.
- **Crop loss:** report within the notified window; the Revenue Department does field verification.
- **House damage:** photographs + Panchayat certificate needed for SDRF compensation.
