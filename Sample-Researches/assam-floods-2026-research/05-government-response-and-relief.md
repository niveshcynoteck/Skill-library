# 5. Government Response & Relief Operations

## 5.1 Rescue operations

- **State Disaster Response Force (SDRF)** and **National Disaster Response Force (NDRF)** deployed across Upper Assam with boats.
- **Indian Army, Air Force and Navy** assisted: Mi-17 and Cheetah helicopters airlifted stranded people; boats and divers used for flood rescue.
- **"Operation Jal Rahat"** (a combined military-civilian flood response) was active in Upper Assam.
- Aerial surveys by the **Chief Minister (26 July)** and the **Union Home Minister (27–29 July)** guided the response; the Home Minister announced a **₹9 lakh ex gratia** package.

## 5.2 Relief camps and distribution centres

- At peak, **89 relief camps / relief distribution centres** were operational.
- **~1.4 lakh people** were sheltered at the peak.
- Supplies: dry ration, drinking water, ORS, medicines, tarpaulins, and animal fodder were distributed.
- As water receded (early August), the focus shifted to **camp closure and safe return**, with many camps still holding displaced families in Sivasagar and Charaideo.

## 5.3 Financial relief to victims

| Scheme | Amount |
|--------|--------|
| Ex gratia for death (state ₹4 lakh + CM Fund ₹5 lakh) | **₹9 lakh per family** |
| Interim cash aid to affected families | **₹15,000** |
| Orunodoi top-up for August | **₹2,500** |
| Crop loss compensation | Per crop-loss norms (after assessment) |
| House damage assistance | Per SDRF norms (fully/partially damaged) |

- **Loan moratorium:** 6-month moratorium for borrowers in **Sivasagar, Charaideo, Jorhat and Golaghat**.

## 5.4 Special packages

- **Sivasagar special relief package** announced by the CM after his aerial survey.
- **PM Relief Fund (PMNRF)** contribution announced for the state.
- **Centre-state coordination:** National Crisis Management Committee (NCMC) reviewed the situation; the Home Minister's visit unlocked central assistance.

## 5.5 Concerns raised

- **Orunodoi beneficiaries** complained of delayed/Aadhaar-linked disbursement glitches.
- **Relief quality** in some camps was criticised (crowding, sanitation).
- **Farmers** demanded better crop-loss compensation and debt waivers — the moratorium partially addressed this.
- **"Dry days"** / low-tide visibility of relief in far-flung char areas was raised by local MLAs.
