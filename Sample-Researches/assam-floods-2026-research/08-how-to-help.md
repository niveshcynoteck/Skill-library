## 8.2 What donations actually help with

| Need | Where it goes |
|------|---------------|
| Cash (best) | CMRF / ASDMA — used for ex gratia, rations, medicines |
| Dry ration / packaged water | Relief distribution centres (coordinate first) |
| ORS, water-purification tablets | Medical posts in camps |
| Tarpaulins, ropes, candles | For families returning to damaged homes |
| Boat transport for evacuation | Through SDRF/NDRF coordination |
| Animal fodder | Veterinary camps in Sivasagar/Charaideo |

## 8.3 NGOs working on the ground

Only give to organisations with proven in-state presence and public audited accounts. Well-known actors in Assam flood relief include **ASDMA-partnered NGOs**, the **Indian Red Cross Society (Assam)**, and established regional bodies such as **AAN (Aaranyak)** for rescue/wildlife, and district-level **Mahila Samitis** running camp kitchens. Always verify credentials before donating to crowd-funding appeals.

## 8.4 Volunteering

- **Join via recognised channels** (ASDMA volunteers, Red Cross, university NSS units) — self-deployed volunteers can obstruct rescue.
- **Skills in demand:** boatmen, medics/nurses, data entry for beneficiary lists, psychologists/counsellors, sanitation engineers.
- **Post-flood (now is the time):** camp-cleaning, house-repair help, well-cleaning and children's education catch-up camps.

## 8.5 Red flags to avoid scams

- Anyone asking for donations in **cash to personal accounts** or UPI IDs that are not the CMRF.
- "Expedite your ex gratia" callers requesting bank details/OTPs — government never asks for these.
- Social-media posts with **unverified casualty numbers** and panic messages — verify on ASDMA and district pages.
