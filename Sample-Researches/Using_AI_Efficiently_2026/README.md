## Summary

Efficient AI use in 2026 is less about picking "the best" model and more about systems thinking: routing tasks to the right model, engineering context (not just prompts), orchestrating multi-agent workflows, and layering in caching/batching to control cost. On the human side, the key differentiator is AI literacy combined with critical thinking — the ability to direct, verify, and govern AI output rather than blindly trust it.
