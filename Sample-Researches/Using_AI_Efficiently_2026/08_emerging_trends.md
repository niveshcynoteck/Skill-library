# Emerging Trends & Future Outlook

- **Small Language Models (SLMs)** are rising fast: 1–13B parameter models delivering strong performance at a fraction of the compute cost (e.g., reports of a ~3.8B model outperforming models 40x its size on some tasks).
- **On-device/local AI**: practitioner reports suggest 80–90% of steps in agentic workflows can now be handled locally/cheaply rather than via frontier cloud models — improving cost, latency, and privacy.
- **Market growth**: the SLM market was ~$7.76B in 2023, projected to reach ~$20.7B by 2030.
- **Outlook**: by 2027–2028, on-device AI is expected to approach today's frontier cloud capability for most everyday tasks, driven by quantization and distillation advances.
