# Agentic Workflows & Multi-Agent Orchestration

2026 is the year "agent orchestration" moved from experiment to enterprise infrastructure:

- **Multi-agent systems** split work across specialized agents (research, analysis, reporting) coordinated by an orchestrator — enabling parallelism and specialization.
- Multi-agent orchestration shows **~30% higher efficiency** than single-agent deployments.
- **40% of enterprise applications** now integrate autonomous agents, and nearly half of European AI pilots have reached production.
- **Leading orchestration platforms**: LangGraph, CrewAI, Microsoft Copilot Studio, n8n, and Coworker.

**Practical implication:** efficient AI use increasingly means designing workflows (who does what, in what order, with what handoffs) rather than writing one clever prompt.
