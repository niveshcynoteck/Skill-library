# Governance, Safety & Responsible Use

Regulation caught up significantly by 2026:

- **EU AI Act (Article 14)** and the **NIST AI Risk Management Framework** both require demonstrable, provable human oversight — not just a checkbox.
- **ISO/IEC 42001** has become a common enterprise compliance reference alongside these.
- **Human-in-the-loop (HITL)** checkpoints are now required for workflows affecting safety, legal rights, or financial outcomes; high-risk systems need drift monitoring, input validation, and rollback procedures.
- **Progress signal**: the share of businesses with *no* responsible-AI policy fell from 24% to 11%, and dedicated AI governance roles grew 17% in 2025.

**Practical implication:** "efficient" in 2026 doesn't mean "fully autonomous" — the most mature organizations pair automation with clear, auditable human checkpoints.
