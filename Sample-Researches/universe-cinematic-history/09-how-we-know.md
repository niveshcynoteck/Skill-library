# How We Know: The Three Pillars of Evidence

The Big Bang isn't a guess — it rests on three pillars of observation [10]:

1. **The expansion of the universe.** Edwin Hubble (1929) showed galaxies are flying apart; space itself is stretching [9][10].
2. **The cosmic microwave background.** The predicted leftover glow of the Big Bang was detected in 1964 and measured with exquisite precision by COBE, WMAP, and Planck [10][14].
3. **The light-element abundances.** The universe is ~75% hydrogen / ~25% helium — exactly the ratio the Big Bang's first three minutes should have produced, and nothing else explains it [10].
