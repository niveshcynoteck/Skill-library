# The Film in One Frame (Visual Timeline)

The full story of the universe compressed into a single timeline. Renders as a Mermaid diagram on GitHub, GitLab, VS Code, Typora, and other Mermaid-enabled viewers.

```mermaid
timeline
    title The Story of the Universe — 13.8 Billion Years
    13.8 billion years ago : The Big Bang : Space inflates ~10^26 times in a split second
    1 second old : Particle soup at 10 billion °C : Matter barely beats antimatter
    3 minutes old : Nucleosynthesis : Hydrogen and helium are born
    380,000 years old : Recombination : Universe becomes transparent : The CMB glow is released
    100-200 million years old : Cosmic Dawn : First stars ignite, ending the Dark Ages
    1 billion years old : Galaxies assemble : Reionization complete
    4.6 billion years ago : Solar System forms : Earth, then the Moon
    5 billion years ago : Dark energy takes over : Expansion accelerates
    Today : The Milky Way, our Sun, and us
    100 billion years ahead : Distant galaxies fade away
    100 trillion years ahead : Last stars flicker out
    10^100 years ahead : Black holes evaporate : Heat death
```

## Timeline Notes

- The time axis is not to scale — the first billion years contain most of the plot, the following 13.8 billion years are the present day, and the final entries stretch into the far future.
- Estimates such as "100–200 million years old" for the first stars represent current scientific ranges, not exact dates.
- The far-future milestones are projections based on our best current physics and could change if dark energy turns out to behave unexpectedly.
