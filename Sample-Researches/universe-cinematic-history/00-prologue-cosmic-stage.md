# Prologue — The Cosmic Stage (Setting the Scene)

Before the story begins, meet the stage and its players.

**The stage:** The universe is **about 13.8 billion years old**. The part of it we can see — the *observable universe* — is roughly **93 billion light-years across**. The full universe may be far larger; we simply cannot know its true size [1][6].

**The players — three kinds of "stuff":**

| Component | Share of the universe | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **Normal matter** | ~5% (4.9%) | Everything you can touch: atoms, stars, planets, gas, you |
| **Dark matter** | ~27% (26.8%) | Invisible; holds galaxies together via gravity; the "glue" of the cosmos |
| **Dark energy** | ~68% (68.3%) | Mysterious force pushing space apart, accelerating expansion |

[2][3][4][5]

**One shocking fact to set the tone:** Everything we can see — all stars, galaxies, and life — is built from less than **5%** of the universe. The other 95% is invisible and still unexplained. The universe's two most important ingredients are the two we understand the least [2][4].
