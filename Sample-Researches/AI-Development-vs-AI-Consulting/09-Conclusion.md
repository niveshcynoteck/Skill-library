# 8. Conclusion — When Each Is Most Suitable

| Your situation | Choose |
|---|---|
| Unclear use cases, no strategy, regulated industry, need board-level clarity | **AI consulting** |
| Defined requirements, validated use cases, AI core to your product, need shipped software | **AI development** |
| Need speed + low risk in the short term (first 12–18 months) | **AI consulting / partner** |
| AI is your product and differentiator; need long-term compounding control | **AI development (often in-house)** |
| Want both strategy and build without handoff loss | **Hybrid** (consult → build) |

Sources: [2][55][60][61]

For an **individual**, the honest framing is temperament: consult if you love conversations, decisions, and client outcomes; develop if you love code, systems, and shipping. The market rewards both handsomely, and increasingly rewards people who can switch hats.
