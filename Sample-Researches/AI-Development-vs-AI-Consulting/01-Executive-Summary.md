# Executive Summary

**AI development** is building — writing code, training and integrating models, deploying and maintaining working AI systems. **AI consulting** is advising — diagnosing problems, setting strategy, selecting vendors, and deciding what to build (or not build). The core distinction, echoed across many sources: a consultant answers *"what should we build and why?"* while a developer answers *"how do we build it?"* [1][2][3].

This is a comparison of two **career paths and business models**, and they are not mutually exclusive. The market is moving toward **hybrids** — consultancies that also build, and engineers who also advise [4][5][6]. Below, the two paths are compared across seven areas, with data validated as of August 2026.
