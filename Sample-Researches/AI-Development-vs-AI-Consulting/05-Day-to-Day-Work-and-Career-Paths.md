# 4. Day-to-Day Work & Career Paths

## AI developer

**Typical week:** ~30% building RAG/agent pipelines, ~15% evaluation and quality checks, ~15% debugging production LLM issues, ~20% meetings/code reviews. Junior engineers spend ~60–70% of time hands-on; that share falls as seniority rises [12][37].

**Career ladder (US, base salary):**

| Rung | Years | Base salary | Scope |
|---|---|---|---|
| Junior / Entry | 0–2 | $110K–$145K | Prompt chains, small retrieval features under review |
| Mid-level | 3–5 | $155K–$215K | Own an entire AI feature, eval harness, token bill |
| Senior | 6–9 | $200K–$290K | Agent/system design, reliability at scale |
| Staff / Principal (IC track) | 10+ | $260K–$400K+ | Org-wide AI direction, hardest problems |
| Manager / Applied AI Lead | 8+ | $260K–$420K+ | Team, roadmap, business outcome |

Source: [38]

Two diverging tracks after senior: stay hands-on (staff/principal) or move into management. Both now pay comparably — the choice is temperament, not money [38].

## AI consultant

**Typical week:** intake calls with prospects, use-case mapping with client operations teams, stakeholder presentations translating model results into adoption language, vendor evaluation, implementation oversight, and business development. Success metric: **"did the client's business problem get solved?"** [31][39].

**Career progression:** analyst → data scientist / ML engineer → junior consultant / internal AI lead → senior / principal consultant (around year 6) → partner or fractional Chief AI Officer [30][40].

**Emerging hybrid roles:**

- **Fractional Chief AI Officer** — part-time executive AI leadership ($8K–$25K/month, or $48K–$120K/year), popular for $5M–$50M companies that cannot justify a full-time AI executive [21][40].
- **Forward-deployed engineer (FDE)** — an engineer embedded on-site with clients to drive implementation and adoption; a role McKinsey highlights as core to the AI-era go-to-market model [54].
- **Engineer-consultant** — one person who runs the discovery diagnostic *and* writes the production code [5].
