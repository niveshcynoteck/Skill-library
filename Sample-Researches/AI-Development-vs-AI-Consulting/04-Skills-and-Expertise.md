# 3. Skills & Expertise Required

| Skill area | AI Development | AI Consulting |
|---|---|---|
| **Technical** | Python, ML frameworks (PyTorch/TensorFlow), LLM/RAG/agents, cloud (AWS/Azure/GCP), MLOps, vector DBs | High AI literacy; enough technical depth to judge feasibility — not production code |
| **Core strength** | Code-first, systems thinking, debugging, evaluation | Diagnostic fluency, use-case mapping, ROI modelling |
| **Business** | Secondary | Primary: stakeholder management, communication, change management |
| **Regulatory** | Compliance-aware implementation | EU AI Act, GDPR, ISO 42001, governance frameworks |

Sources: [28][29][30][31][32]

## AI developer skills

- **Python** is non-negotiable — it appears in nearly 100% of AI engineering job postings [28][33].
- Software engineering fundamentals: APIs, version control, testing, debugging, shipping and maintaining real applications.
- Retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), evaluation harnesses, agent orchestration, guardrails, cost control, and production deployment (cloud, monitoring, observability) [28][36].
- ML foundations: PyTorch/TensorFlow, linear algebra, probability, statistics — enough to debug and choose models intelligently [28].

## AI consultant skills

- **"Translation fluency"** — converting a business problem into an AI feasibility decision and communicating tradeoffs to non-technical executives. Cited as the threshold skill for consulting [31][34].
- Use-case mapping (from 30 ideas, pick the 3 that are feasible, valuable, and politically deliverable), ROI modelling, change management, stakeholder and executive communication [30].
- Regulatory literacy: EU AI Act, GDPR, ISO 42001 — increasingly an entry ticket for European AI engagements [30].
- Technical depth is still required, but as *judgment*: enough to assess feasibility honestly and "call BS on a vendor pitch", not to write production code [3][30].

## Typical routes in

- **Developers:** often transition from software engineering; a focused **6–12 months** of side projects (LLM app → RAG → agents/production) is a realistic path into a mid-level AI role [36].
- **Consultants:** typically spend **5–7 years** in data-analyst, data-scientist, or ML-engineer roles before moving into consulting [30]. A master's degree is effectively expected at top consultancies [30].

## What does NOT differentiate you

- Raw, recently-public AI techniques (prompt engineering, fine-tuning, RAG assembly) **commoditize within 12–18 months**; they become table stakes. Vertical domain expertise and a client-delivery track record commoditize far more slowly [31].
- Credentials matter less than a portfolio of shipped systems (developers) or documented engagements (consultants) [31][36].
