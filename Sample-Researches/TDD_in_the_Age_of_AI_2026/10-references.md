# References

All references below were link-checked (HTTP 200) prior to publication. See [`validated_links.csv`](./validated_links.csv) for the full validation table. Statistics are attributed to their original survey or study; where a figure could not be independently cross-verified beyond a single source, it is presented as reported by that source.

1. **DevAssure** — ["Why TDD Is Having a Second Act in the Age of AI Coding Agents"](https://www.devassure.io/blog/tdd-second-act-ai-coding-agents/). Core argument for TDD preventing agents from validating their own broken implementations.
2. **DeviQA** — ["State of AI-Generated Code 2026: The QA and Testing Gap"](https://www.deviqa.com/blog/state-of-ai-generated-code-2026-the-qa-and-testing-gap/). Survey of 300 QA professionals on bug volume and trust.
3. **Thoughtworks** — [Technology Radar, Volume 34 (April 2026)](https://www.thoughtworks.com/about-us/news/2026/combat-ai-cognitive-debt-radar-v34). Industry radar on AI cognitive debt and mutation testing as an Adopt-level practice.
4. **Augment Code** — ["What Is Spec-Driven Development?"](https://www.augmentcode.com/guides/what-is-spec-driven-development). Background on SDD as a response to AI "vibe coding" drift.
5. **Augment Code** — ["Spec + TDD: The Combination That Actually Produces Shippable AI Code"](https://www.augmentcode.com/guides/spec-tdd-shippable-ai-generated-code). The five-phase Spec+TDD workflow referenced in Figure 3.
6. **Augment Code** — ["Mutation Testing for AI-Generated Code"](https://www.augmentcode.com/guides/mutation-testing-ai-generated-code). Practical guide to verifying AI-generated test suite quality.
7. **The Pragmatic Engineer** — ["TDD, AI Agents and Coding with Kent Beck"](https://newsletter.pragmaticengineer.com/p/tdd-ai-agents-and-coding-with-kent). Primary interview source for Kent Beck's views and quotes.
8. **Alonso, Yovine & Braberman** — ["Test-Driven Agentic Development" (arXiv, 2026)](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2603.17973). Academic paper on dependency-graph-based test impact analysis for coding agents.
9. **Forbes Technology Council** — ["The Most Ignored Practice in AI Coding: Test-Driven Development"](https://www.forbes.com/councils/forbestechcouncil/2026/04/15/the-most-ignored-practice-in-ai-coding-test-driven-development/). Source of the Boris Cherny quote and the economic case for verification.
10. **Sonar** — ["State of Code Developer Survey: The Current Reality of AI Coding" (2026)](https://www.sonarsource.com/blog/state-of-code-developer-survey-report-the-current-reality-of-ai-coding/). Survey of 1,100+ developers; source of the 42%/96%/48% statistics.
11. **Sonar** — [Press release on the AI "verification gap"](https://www.sonarsource.com/company/press-releases/sonar-data-reveals-critical-verification-gap-in-ai-coding/). Confirms the 48% always-verify statistic.
12. **Stack Overflow Blog** — ["Closing the Developer AI Trust Gap"](https://stackoverflow.blog/2026/02/18/closing-the-developer-ai-trust-gap/). Source of the 2023–2025 adoption vs. trust figures used in Figure 1.
13. **CodeRabbit** — ["State of AI vs. Human Code Generation" Report](https://www.coderabbit.ai/blog/state-of-ai-vs-human-code-generation-report). Analysis of 470 open-source pull requests; source of Figure 2's defect-rate data.
14. **GitHub Blog** — ["Test-Driven Development (TDD) with GitHub Copilot"](https://github.blog/ai-and-ml/github-copilot/github-for-beginners-test-driven-development-tdd-with-github-copilot/). Official guide to practicing TDD with Copilot.
15. **DEV Community** — ["TDD with AI: Claude Writes Tests First, Then the Implementation"](https://dev.to/spyrae/tdd-with-ai-claude-writes-tests-first-then-the-implementation-27hm). Practical walkthrough of test-first prompting with Claude.

**Note:** A CodeRabbit press release originally cited via Businesswire returned an unreachable status during link validation and was replaced with CodeRabbit's own blog post (#13) covering the same report.
