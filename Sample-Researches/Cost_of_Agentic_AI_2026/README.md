## Methodology

Each section was researched independently, prioritizing official vendor documentation (Anthropic, OpenAI, Google) and reputable analyst/research sources (Gartner, IDC, McKinsey, Bloomberg Intelligence, Epoch AI, a16z), with important claims cross-verified across multiple sources. All reference URLs collected across the report (~75 total) were programmatically validated for reachability, with manual spot-checks on any 403/404/000 results to distinguish bot-blocking from genuine breakage. Corrections made during validation, and figures that could not be independently verified, are documented in [09_source_verification_notes.md](09_source_verification_notes.md). Claims of uncertain reliability (vendor-blog statistics not corroborated by primary research) are explicitly flagged as such within each section rather than presented as settled fact.
