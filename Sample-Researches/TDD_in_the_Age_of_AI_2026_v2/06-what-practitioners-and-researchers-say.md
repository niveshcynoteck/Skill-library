## 6. What Practitioners and Researchers Say

### The consensus forming in 2026

- **Thoughtworks** (25th anniversary of the Agile Manifesto workshop, reported by The Register, Feb 2026): *"TDD produces dramatically better results from AI coding agents."* Under Chatham House rules, senior practitioners went further: **"TDD has never been more important."**
- **Jason Gorman** (independent practitioner): developers are "discovering eXtreme Programming and attributing the benefits to chatbots" — test-first thinking, small steps, continuous testing are being *rediscovered* because they make AI agents work. He estimates only **~8% of ~1,500 developers** surveyed actually practice TDD.
- **Google Cloud/DORA**: TDD principles amplify AI success; vision combined with TDD practices produces better AI results.
- **QA Skills / Full Scale / Sonar**: AI has made *testing a bigger job*, not a smaller one — the industry's #1 concern is now "looks correct but is unreliable" (53% cite it as their top technical-debt worry).

### The data on trust

- Stack Overflow's AI-trust research: developer trust in AI accuracy **fell from 40% to 29%** year-over-year despite rising adoption.
- DORA 2025: ~30% of developers trust AI outputs **little or not at all**.
- Sonar: only **48%** of developers always verify AI code before committing.
