# Is the Ramayana Historically True? — Research Overview

This research examines the claims and evidence around whether the events of the Ramayana are historically true. It weighs archaeological findings, geological studies, astronomical dating attempts, and textual scholarship against mainstream historical and scientific consensus, and concludes with the researcher's own assessment.

**Framing note:** "Proof" of a religious epic's historicity is a contested area where devout claims, archaeological findings, and mainstream historical/scientific scholarship often disagree. This research presents all sides — what's claimed as evidence, what mainstream historians/archaeologists/scientists say, and where the debate stands — rather than asserting the epic is factually "proven" true or false.
