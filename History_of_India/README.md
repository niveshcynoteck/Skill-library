# History of India — Research Report

A structured research report covering the history of India from the Indus Valley Civilization to the present day.

## Contents

1. [01_ancient_india.md](01_ancient_india.md) — Indus Valley Civilization, Vedic Period, rise of Buddhism/Jainism/Hinduism (c. 3300 BCE – 6th century BCE)
2. [02_classical_early_medieval_india.md](02_classical_early_medieval_india.md) — Mauryan Empire, Gupta "Golden Age," regional dynasties (Pallavas, Chalukyas, Rashtrakutas, Cholas) (c. 322 BCE – 12th century CE)
3. [03_medieval_india.md](03_medieval_india.md) — Delhi Sultanate, Vijayanagara Empire, Bahmani Sultanate, Rajput kingdoms (1206–1526)
4. [04_mughal_empire.md](04_mughal_empire.md) — Founding, golden age, administration, art & architecture, and decline (1526–1857)
5. [05_colonial_era.md](05_colonial_era.md) — European arrival, East India Company, British Raj policies, socio-economic impact (1498–1947)
6. [06_independence_movement.md](06_independence_movement.md) — Indian National Congress, key leaders, major movements, Partition (1885–1947)
7. [07_post_independence_india.md](07_post_independence_india.md) — Constitution, wars, political/economic milestones (1947–present)
8. [08_source_verification_notes.md](08_source_verification_notes.md) — Methodology and link-validation results for all sources cited

## Methodology

Each section was researched independently with cross-verification of key facts (especially dates) across at least two sources per claim, prioritizing official/encyclopedic sources (Britannica, World History Encyclopedia, government portals, Wikipedia). All ~120 reference URLs collected across the report were programmatically validated for reachability; results and corrections are documented in [08_source_verification_notes.md](08_source_verification_notes.md). Facts that are genuinely disputed or uncertain among historians (e.g., exact birth/death dates of the Buddha and Mahavira, Partition death tolls, the "drain of wealth" theory) are explicitly flagged as such within the relevant sections rather than presented as settled fact.
